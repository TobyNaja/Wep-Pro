Top Biggest Animals

Animals take many shapes and forms and come in a great diversity of sizes, from microscopic to the 30 m long blue whale! Whether you measure in terms of weight, height or length, there is no denying some animals are huge in comparison to others in their class.

1. Blue Whale

The Blue whale is the largest animal of all time.

2. Colossal Squid

The size of the colossal squid is often exaggerated.

3. African Elephant

The largest living land animal on Earth.

4. Giraffe

The tallest living animal, reaching up to 5.8 meters.
